Thursday 26 November 2026, folded into a single 1024×1024 picture: 45
stories that happened or were first reported that day, grouped under seven
region headings — Middle East, Europe, North America, South America,
Africa, Asia and Oceania.

The lead story is Washington's new tariff round against 14 trading partners
(40% on China, 35% on Vietnam, 25% on India), drawn as a container ship
under hanging price tags. Around it: Houthi strikes on Saudi Arabia's
Ras Tanura refinery, Ukraine damaging five Russian jets, Kim Jong Un
calling for wider tactical-nuclear deployment, a Pakistani astronaut
joining China's Tiangong station, a Cairo ring-road fire that killed 31,
and 39 more.

`build_image.py` draws the whole page from scratch with Python PIL (no
libraries beyond Pillow, no network); `main.ts` checks the PNG signature
and copies `entry.png` to the output folder.
